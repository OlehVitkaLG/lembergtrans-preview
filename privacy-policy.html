Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Who we are

Lemberg Group Inc, 1141 E Main St, Unit 109, East Dundee, IL 60118. USDOT 2395323. Questions about this policy: dispatch@lembergtrans.com.

What we collect

  • Information you submit through the rate request or contact form: name, company, email, phone and the load details you choose to share.
  • Technical data collected automatically: IP address, browser type, pages viewed and referring source, through cookies and analytics tools.

Why we use it

  • To price, book and move your freight, and to communicate with you about it.
  • To keep records required of a motor carrier under federal and state regulation.
  • To understand how the website is used so we can improve it.

What we do not do

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. Information is shared only with service providers who help us operate — for example our insurance agent when you request a certificate of insurance.

Cookies and analytics

[Analytics tools actually installed — GA4, Google Tag Manager, Meta Pixel.] You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries for as long as we are in contact with you about the freight, and shipping records for as long as the record-keeping rules that apply to a motor carrier require. After that we delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, except where we are required to keep records by law. Write to dispatch@lembergtrans.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date at the top.
