About Lemberg Group

A trucking company that has operated under the same DOT number, from the same Illinois office, since 2012.

Who we are

Fourteen Years, One Authority, One Address

Lemberg Group Inc was founded in 2012 in East Dundee, Illinois. We started with a small fleet moving dry van freight out of the Chicago market, and we have been doing the same thing ever since — with more trucks, more lanes, the same operating authority and the same ownership since day one.

In an industry where carriers appear, collect a few loads and dissolve, longevity is not a marketing line. It is the first thing a broker checks. USDOT 2395323 has been active and in our name since day one, and anyone can verify that in the FMCSA database in about ten seconds.

We are asset-based: the trucks are ours, the drivers are ours, and the dispatchers answering the phone sit in our own office. Nothing gets handed to a stranger.

How we operate

Four Things We Do Not Compromise On

We answer honestly

If we cannot cover your load, we say so in the first call instead of holding the freight hostage while we look for a truck.

We stay compliant

Authority, insurance, driver files and ELD records are kept current — not scrambled together when a customer asks.

We maintain early

Preventive maintenance costs less than a missed appointment and a load sitting on the shoulder of I-80.

We keep our drivers

Drivers who stay learn your facilities. Carriers with constant turnover send a stranger to your dock every week.

Dispatch

One Team, Around the Clock

Our dispatch desk is staffed 24/7 out of East Dundee. That matters at 2 a.m. on a Saturday, when a truck has a problem 900 miles from home and someone has to decide what happens to your freight.

  • A named contact on recurring accounts, not a shared queue.
  • Proactive updates at pickup, en route and delivery.
  • Escalation path that reaches an owner, not a call center.
