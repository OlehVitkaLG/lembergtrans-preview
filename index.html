FAQ

Questions Brokers Ask Us First

Are you a carrier or a broker?+

A carrier. We own the trucks and employ the drivers, and we operate under our own authority, USDOT 2395323. We do not post your freight back to a load board.

What kind of freight do you haul?+

General freight in 53' dry vans — dry food, machinery, paper, packaging, palletized and floor-loaded. Through our owner-operator capacity we also move open deck and reefer freight, and we load LTL. We do not carry hazmat in any placarded class.

Where do you run?+

All 48 continental states, over-the-road, from a home base in Chicagoland. The honest mix: roughly 70% Midwest and South, 20% East, about 10% West. We do not run into New York City.

How quickly can you cover a load?+

Send us the lane and a dispatcher checks available capacity right away. You get an answer either way — including a straight no if we cannot cover it, so you are not left waiting.

Can you send a certificate of insurance?+

Yes. Send your request to dispatch@lembergtrans.com and our agent issues the COI directly to your compliance contact.
