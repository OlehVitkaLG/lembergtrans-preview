Truckload Services

One asset-based carrier for spot loads, recurring lanes and staged trailers — with the same dispatch team behind all three. Dry van is our core; open deck, reefer and LTL move through our owner-operator capacity.

Service 01

OTR Dry Van Truckload

Full truckload service in 53' dry vans across all 48 states. This is the core of what we do and what our fleet is built for: general commodity freight moving long haul, on appointment, with electronic tracking from pickup to delivery.

  • Palletized and floor-loaded freight — consumer goods, packaged food, paper, building products, retail and e-commerce volume.
  • Live load, live unload, or drop — we work to your facility's process, not the other way around.
  • Appointment management — the driver's hours are planned around your dock window, so the truck arrives inside it.
  • Loaded and empty calls pushed to you automatically.
Service 02

Dedicated & Contract Capacity

If the same lane shows up on your board every week, re-sourcing it every week is wasted effort and unpredictable cost. Dedicated capacity means agreed trucks, agreed volume and an agreed rate for a defined term.

  • Committed weekly loads on named origin–destination pairs.
  • Contract pricing instead of riding the spot market up and down.
  • A named dispatcher who knows your facilities, your receivers and your quirks.
  • Volume reviewed monthly, so capacity follows your season instead of fighting it.
Service 03

Drop-and-Hook & Trailer Pools

Trailers staged at your facility so loading happens on your schedule, not while a driver's clock runs in your yard. For high-volume docks this is usually the single fastest way to cut detention.

  • Trailer pool sized to your outbound volume.
  • Preloaded outbound — the driver hooks and leaves.
  • Lower detention exposure on both sides of the move.
  • Works alongside dedicated lanes as one combined program.
