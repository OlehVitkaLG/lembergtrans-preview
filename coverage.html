Where We Run

All 48 continental states, over the road, from a home base in the Chicago freight market.

Home market

Chicagoland In, Chicagoland Out

Our terminal sits in East Dundee, Illinois — roughly 40 miles northwest of downtown Chicago and inside the largest inland freight hub in North America. That location is the reason we can put a truck on a Midwest pickup faster than a carrier repositioning from three states away.

  • Northern Illinois — Chicago, Elgin, Aurora, Joliet, Rockford
  • Southern Wisconsin — Kenosha, Racine, Milwaukee
  • Northwest Indiana — Gary, Hammond, Portage
  • Eastern Iowa and the I-80 corridor

Transit planning

Hours of Service Are Built Into the Quote

A 1,800-mile lane is not a two-day lane just because the math says so. We plan transit around legal driving hours and your dock windows, then quote the date we can actually hit. If the appointment needs a team, we tell you before you book, not after.

  • Solo transit — up to roughly 500–600 miles per driving day
  • Appointment-based delivery planned against available hours
  • We stay out of New York City — everything around it we run.

Asset-based OTR dry van truckload carrier operating in all 48 states since 2012. Company-owned fleet, company drivers, in-house dispatch.
