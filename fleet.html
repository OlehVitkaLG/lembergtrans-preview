Trucks

The Trucks We Own

Our trucks are spec'd for long-haul dry van work and cycled out before they become a reliability problem. Newer equipment is not a comfort feature — it is the difference between a load that arrives on the appointment and one that does not.

  • Model years 2023–2026, replaced on a rolling schedule
  • Pre-pass on about 70% of the fleet — fewer weigh-station stops, tighter transit.
  • ELD and GPS on every unit, so available hours are checked before we commit to your appointment.

Maintenance

Preventive, Not Reactive

Every unit runs a scheduled preventive maintenance program, and every driver files a pre-trip and post-trip inspection. Defects go to the shop before dispatch, not after a roadside inspection finds them.

  • Scheduled preventive maintenance on every unit, tracked in-house.
  • DOT annual inspections tracked per unit
  • Driver vehicle inspection reports reviewed daily
  • Tire and brake programs managed centrally, not truck by truck
  • Our own shop in East Dundee — maintenance and repairs are handled in-house.
