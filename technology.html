Technology & Transparency

You should never have to call and ask where your freight is. Our systems connect to the ones you already use, and you get the same view of the load that our dispatcher has.

Transparency

We Run an Open Book on Every Load

Most freight problems are not caused by a truck being late. They are caused by nobody telling you it would be late. We solve that by giving you access instead of updates on request.

  • Live tracking on every load — location straight from the truck's ELD, not from a dispatcher's guess.
  • Automatic status events — dispatched, at pickup, loaded, in transit, at delivery, empty.
  • Documents the same day — signed POD and BOL scanned from the road, not mailed next week.
  • One escalation path that ends at an owner of the company.
  • Our compliance file is always current — authority, insurance and driver records ready before you ask for them.
Our stack

What Runs Behind the Truck

Every load is planned, tracked, documented and invoiced through connected systems — not a spreadsheet and a phone.

Transportation management system

Loads, drivers, trailers, appointments and settlements live in one TMS, so nothing depends on which dispatcher happens to be on shift.

ELD & GPS on 100% of the fleet

Electronic logs on every truck. Available driving hours are checked against your appointment before we accept the load, not after.

Tablet with unlimited data in every truck for ELD, logs and paperwork.

Real-time visibility

We share tracking through the visibility platform your team already works in — no new login, no new process on your side.

EDI & API connections

Tenders, status messages and invoices can move system to system instead of by email — 204, 214, 210 and 990 where your side supports it.

Carrier onboarding platforms

Our authority, insurance and safety data are monitored and kept current in the onboarding systems brokers use to vet carriers.

Digital documents & billing

PODs scanned in the cab and attached to the invoice automatically, so your AP team gets one complete package instead of chasing paperwork.
